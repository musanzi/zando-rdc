# Route supplier inquiries through the Zando team

Zando RDC provides verified local supplier discovery, with the Zando team handling customer inquiries through WhatsApp. Visitors browse supplier profiles by town and category, then contact the team with the selected supplier's details. This keeps the Zando team as the customer's contact point.

Customers and suppliers do not need accounts. Accounts belong to administrators and ambassadors, who manage supplier information on behalf of suppliers. Ambassadors submit suppliers for administrator review and approval. Suppliers must pass documented checks before publication, including identity, location, and business activity.

The public catalog presents supplier profiles with examples of their offerings. Discovery is free. Prices, availability, and subsequent commercial arrangements are handled outside the application.

Supplier contact information and verification documents remain private. The public profile shows the supplier's town and neighborhood, and its contact action directs visitors to the Zando team with the supplier's name and profile link.

The application measures WhatsApp contact clicks but does not track customer requests, deals, payments, bookings, or commissions. The product scope ends at supplier discovery and the WhatsApp contact action. Commercial processes are outside this brief.
