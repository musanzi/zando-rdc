# Zando RDC

Zando RDC connects customers with local suppliers. Visitors discover suppliers through Zando and contact the Zando team on WhatsApp.

## Language

**Supplier**:
A person or business that customers can discover through Zando RDC.
_Avoid_: Vendor, seller

**Visitor**:
A person who browses Zando RDC to discover suppliers.

**Customer**:
A person or organization looking for a supplier through Zando RDC.
_Avoid_: Client, buyer

**Ambassador**:
A Zando representative who submits suppliers for administrator review and approval.

**Administrator**:
A person with full management authority over Zando RDC.
_Avoid_: Admin

**Zando team**:
The people who handle customer contact through Zando's WhatsApp channel.

**Supplier profile**:
A supplier's presentation on Zando RDC, including its location, categories, photos, and examples of its offerings.
_Avoid_: Product listing, store

**Base location**:
The place where a supplier is based.

**Service town**:
A town or city where a supplier offers its goods or services.

**Category**:
A grouping of supplier offerings that customers use to find relevant suppliers.

**Contact click**:
A visitor's activation of a supplier profile's WhatsApp contact button for the Zando team. It does not establish that a message was sent or a deal was made.
_Avoid_: Lead, booking, sale

**Verified supplier**:
A supplier whose identity, location, and business activity have passed Zando's documented checks and administrator approval. Verification describes those checks and does not guarantee a future transaction.

**Verification evidence**:
Private information supporting a supplier review, including contact confirmation, a location check, and photos of the business or its work.

**Estimated unique WhatsApp contacts**:
The estimated number of distinct browsers that activate a supplier's WhatsApp contact button during a selected period. This measures contact clicks, not confirmed conversations.
